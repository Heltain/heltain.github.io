Call Guard Privacy Policy

Heltain

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Call Guard is designed to screen incoming phone calls while minimizing access to personal information. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not require an account.

Internet access

Call Guard does not request Android's Internet permission. Call screening and contact matching are performed locally on your device.

Call information

Android provides Call Guard with information needed to evaluate eligible incoming calls through Android's Call Screening service. Call Guard uses this information locally to decide whether an eligible call should be allowed or blocked according to your settings.

Call Guard does not maintain a history of caller names, phone numbers, call times, or call durations. It does not request permission to read your Android call log.

Contacts

Call Guard's Contacts Only mode does not require permission to read your contacts.

If you choose an area-code screening mode, Call Guard requests permission to read your contacts. This access is necessary so the app can recognize contacts and ensure that people in your contacts remain allowed regardless of the area-code rule you configure.

Contact information is processed locally on your device. Call Guard does not upload, transmit, sell, or share your contacts. You can deny or revoke Contacts permission through Android's permission settings. If the permission is unavailable, Call Guard falls back to Contacts Only behavior for screening.

Information stored by Call Guard

Call Guard stores a small amount of app configuration data on your device. This can include your selected screening mode, configured area codes, pause setting, preferences, and a cumulative count of calls blocked by Call Guard.

The blocked-call counter is a number only. It does not contain caller names, phone numbers, timestamps, or a history of individual calls.

Backup and device migration

Call Guard permits supported Android backup and device-migration services to preserve its app settings. Depending on your Android device and your Google or device backup configuration, locally stored Call Guard settings may therefore be included in an Android-managed backup or transferred to another device.

Call Guard does not operate its own cloud service or server for storing this information.

Data collection and sharing

Heltain does not receive or collect your contacts, caller information, Call Guard settings, or blocked-call counter. Call Guard does not sell personal information or share personal information with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Data security

Call Guard minimizes data exposure by performing its screening functions locally and by not requesting Internet access. Access to contacts is requested only when required for the optional area-code screening modes and is governed by Android's permission system.

Data retention and deletion

App settings remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or the app is uninstalled. The cumulative blocked-call counter can be reset within Call Guard.

Android backup or device-migration services may retain backed-up app settings according to the policies and settings of those services. Heltain does not maintain a separate server-side copy of Call Guard data and therefore has no server-held Call Guard user data to delete.

Children's privacy

Call Guard is a general-purpose utility and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. Heltain does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Call Guard.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Call Guard's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
