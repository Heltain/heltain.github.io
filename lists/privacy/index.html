Lists Privacy Policy

Heltain

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Lists is designed as an offline-first list-making application. The app does not contain advertising or tracking, does not require an account, and does not require Internet access.

Internet access

Lists does not request Android's Internet permission. Your lists and app settings are stored locally on your device.

Information stored by Lists

Lists stores the information you create in the app, including lists, list descriptions, sections, items, notes, item order, checkbox state, recently deleted content, and app preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device. Heltain does not operate a server that receives or stores your lists.

Camera access and QR codes

Lists can request access to your device's camera when you choose to scan a Lists QR code. Camera access is used for QR-code scanning and is not required for ordinary list creation or use.

Lists does not upload camera images or video to Heltain.

Sharing and importing lists

Lists allows you to share list information using Android's standard sharing features and through QR codes. Lists can also import list information from supported text, files, or QR codes when you choose to do so.

Information you intentionally share is provided to the destination or application you select. Heltain does not receive a copy of information you share.

Backup and device migration

Lists permits supported Android backup and device-migration services to preserve its local database. Depending on your Android device and your Google or device backup configuration, your Lists data may therefore be included in an Android-managed backup or transferred to another device.

Heltain does not operate its own cloud backup or synchronization service for Lists.

Data collection and sharing

Heltain does not receive or collect the lists, notes, sections, items, preferences, or QR-code contents you use with Lists. Lists does not sell personal information or share personal information with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

Data security

Lists minimizes data exposure by storing application data locally and by not requesting Internet access. Camera access is controlled through Android's permission system and is used only when you choose to scan a QR code.

Data retention and deletion

Lists data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the app. Recently deleted content may remain available within the app for up to 14 days so that it can be restored.

Android backup or device-migration services may retain backed-up application data according to the policies and settings of those services. Heltain does not maintain a separate server-side copy of your Lists data and therefore has no server-held Lists user data to delete.

Children's privacy

Lists is a general-purpose utility and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. Heltain does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Lists.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Lists' features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
