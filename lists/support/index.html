Lists

Lists is a clean, offline-first Android app for creating and organizing lists without accounts, ads, or unnecessary clutter.

Support

Lists stores your lists on your device. You can create lists and sections, reorder items, add notes, check off items, recover recently deleted content, and share or import lists.

Lists can also use your camera to scan a Lists QR code. Camera access is only requested when you use the QR scanning feature.
