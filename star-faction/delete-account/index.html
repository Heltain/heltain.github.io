Star Faction: Ascension — Pilot Profile Deletion

Heltain

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Star Faction: Ascension (Android package com.heltain.spaceshooter) is an Android space shooter published by Heltain. This page explains how players can permanently delete their Star Faction pilot profile, Firebase cloud identity, and associated game data directly inside the game, or request manual deletion if they no longer have access to the game.

Delete your profile in the game

Players can delete their Star Faction profile directly inside the game at any time by following this path:

Main Menu → PILOT PROFILE → DELETE PILOT PROFILE

  1. Open Star Faction: Ascension on your Android device and go to the Main Menu.
  2. Tap the Pilot Profile bar at the top of the screen (showing your pilot callsign and #XXXX-XXXX player code) to open the PILOT PROFILE menu.
  3. Tap DELETE PILOT PROFILE at the bottom of the menu.
  4. Review the confirmation dialog (DELETE PILOT PROFILE?) and tap DELETE PERMANENTLY.

This permanently deletes your Star Faction cloud identity and associated game data (whether you use an anonymous guest profile or a Google-linked profile), clears that profile's local records from your device, and creates a new anonymous guest profile so the game remains playable.

Request deletion without the game installed

If you no longer have access to the game or the device on which it was installed, you can contact Heltain support by email to request manual deletion of your Star Faction pilot profile.

Heltain Support
Email: heltain.apps@gmail.com

When contacting support to request profile deletion, please include:

  • A clear statement that you are requesting permanent deletion of your Star Faction: Ascension pilot profile.
  • Your pilot callsign (nickname) and player code (#XXXX-XXXX), if available, solely to help locate your profile.
  • If your profile was linked to a Google Account, please send your email from that linked Google Account address if possible.

Account ownership verification: Because pilot callsigns and player codes (#XXXX-XXXX) are publicly visible on leaderboards and in player search, a public player code or callsign alone is not sufficient proof of account ownership. Additional verification may be required before a manual deletion request is completed.

Security notice: Never send passwords, Google login credentials, authentication ID tokens, or other security secrets by email. Heltain will never ask for your password.

What deletion covers

Deleting your Star Faction pilot profile permanently deletes your Star Faction Firebase account and associated cloud records, including:

  • Pilot nickname and player code: Your generated pilot callsign and permanent player code reservation (#XXXX-XXXX).
  • Public player profile: Your player profile record and public search profile used for player discovery.
  • Leaderboard scores and rankings: Your score entries across the Daily, Weekly, and All-Time leaderboards.
  • Following relationships: Your account's Following list of tracked pilots.
  • Cloud achievements: All unlocked cloud achievement records and achievement summary data associated with your profile.
  • Gameplay submission history: Associated run score submission records and submission rate-limit records stored for your profile.
  • Firebase Authentication identity: Your Star Faction Firebase Authentication user record and any link between that profile and Google Sign-In.

Your Google Account is not deleted

Deletion of a Google-linked Star Faction pilot profile does not delete or affect your underlying Google Account. Only the connection between your Google Account and Star Faction, along with the profile's associated Star Faction game data, is removed.

Retention and exceptions

Once profile deletion is completed, your Star Faction Firebase Authentication identity, player profile, player code, leaderboard records, Following list, cloud achievements, and score submission records are permanently deleted from the game's database and cannot be recovered.

If another player previously followed your profile, your deleted public profile and scores no longer exist and will not appear in their Following leaderboard.

Deleting your profile inside the game also clears that profile's local records on your device. If your profile is deleted manually through support while the app is still installed on a device, locally stored device data can be removed by clearing application data or uninstalling the game. Standard short-term cloud infrastructure diagnostic logs are governed by Google Cloud's routine operational retention policies and do not retain an active player profile or leaderboard identity.
