Star Faction: Ascension is a portrait-mode Android space shooter featuring three enemy factions, major boss encounters, weapon upgrades, and endless survival.

Support

If you encounter an installation problem, gameplay bug, or Google Play Games sign-in or leaderboard issue, please include the game version, your Android device model, and a brief description of what happened when contacting us. Do not send passwords or other sensitive account credentials.

Google Play Games

Online leaderboards and achievements require a compatible Google Play Games account and an internet connection. The game remains playable without signing in.
