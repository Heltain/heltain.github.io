Star Faction: Ascension is a fast-paced portrait-mode space shooter built for responsive one-handed play on Android. Game Features

Three Enemy Factions

Battle armored Iron Syndicate warships, aggressive Void Swarm formations, and precision Aether Dominion constructs.

Multi-Phase Bosses

Confront screen-filling capital bosses with destructible subsystems, shield beacons, and evolving attack patterns.

Evolving Weapon Cores

Collect mid-run weapon upgrades, switch firing cores, and deploy autonomous combat drones to survive deeper waves.

Endless Ascension Scaling

Defeating all three faction bosses loops the campaign into higher Ascension cycles with intensified enemy firepower.

Global & Following Leaderboards

Compete across All-Time, Weekly, and Daily rankings, or search by pilot callsign and player code to follow rivals.

Zero Ads or Clutter

Pure arcade gameplay with no advertisements, no energy timers, and full offline playability whenever you are disconnected.

Enemy Factions & Capital Bosses

Each combat sector introduces a new hostile faction culminating in a multi-stage flagship encounter:

  • Sector I — Iron Syndicate
    Heavy Armor • Ballistic Salvos • Flagship: Dreadnought MK-I

    Industrial gunships and interceptor squadrons backed by a heavily plated dreadnought armed with twin stabilizers and sweeping artillery.

  • Sector II — Void Swarm
    High-Speed Flankers • Bio-Plasma Barrages • Flagship: Hive Leviathan

    Relentless razorwing packs and brood carriers commanded by an apex leviathan that spawns escort waves and corrosive spreads.

  • Sector III — Aether Dominion
    Prismatic Beams • Shield Matrix Tech • Flagship: Celestial Arbiter

    Precision prism darts, halo sentinels, and arc weavers guarding the Celestial Arbiter and its rotating orbital shield segments.

Pilot Profiles, Leaderboards & Cloud Sync

Star Faction: Ascension features built-in Pilot Profiles powered by Google Firebase so you can play immediately without mandatory sign-in prompts:

  • Instant Guest Pilot Profile: Every installation automatically receives a curated sci-fi pilot callsign and a unique 8-character player code (#XXXX-XXXX).
  • Optional Google Account Linking: Link your Google Account at any time from the in-game PILOT PROFILE menu to protect and recover your pilot identity across devices.
  • Player Search & Following: Look up other pilots by callsign or player code to build your custom Following leaderboard alongside the Global All-Time, Weekly, and Daily boards.
  • Cloud Achievements & Full Data Control: Unlock 13 combat achievements backed up to the cloud, reroll your callsign whenever you like, or permanently delete your pilot profile and all cloud records directly in-game via Main Menu → PILOT PROFILE → DELETE PILOT PROFILE (or learn more on our Pilot Profile Deletion page).
