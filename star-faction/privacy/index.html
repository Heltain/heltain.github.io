Star Faction: Ascension Privacy Policy

Heltain

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Star Faction: Ascension (Android package com.heltain.spaceshooter) is an Android space shooter published by Heltain. This policy explains how the game handles gameplay records, Firebase-backed online competitive features (pilot profiles, optional Google account linking, leaderboards, player search, Following, and achievements), usage analytics, and profile deletion.

Pilot profiles and optional Google account linking

You can play Star Faction: Ascension without providing your name, email address, or password. When online services initialize, the game uses Google Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous guest pilot profile with a randomly generated sci-fi callsign (nickname) and a permanent player code formatted as #XXXX-XXXX. You can generate a new random callsign at any time from the in-game PILOT PROFILE menu while keeping your player code and records.

Optionally, you may link your pilot profile to your Google Account using Google Sign-In (Main Menu → PILOT PROFILE → LINK GOOGLE ACCOUNT) so that you can recover your pilot profile, leaderboard records, Following list, and cloud achievements if you switch devices or reinstall the game. When you link a Google Account, Firebase Authentication associates your Google account identifier with your Star Faction profile to authenticate and recover your account.

Public vs. private profile information

  • Publicly visible to other players: Your generated pilot callsign (nickname), your permanent player code (#XXXX-XXXX), and your submitted leaderboard scores, highest wave reached, and rankings are visible to other players on leaderboards and through in-game player search (FIND PILOT).
  • Private and internal account information: Linked Google Account email addresses, real names, and Google account identifiers are not displayed publicly to other players, nor are your Following list of tracked pilots, your individual cloud achievement unlock records, or your local device settings. Your Firebase Authentication user ID (UID) is an internal account identifier used by the application's backend and is not intentionally displayed as a player-facing identity in the game interface, though it may be used in underlying backend document identifiers or service responses that support leaderboards and player discovery.

Gameplay records, leaderboards, Following, and achievements

The game stores gameplay records such as your high score, highest wave reached, unlocked achievements, Boss Core progression, and settings on your device. If you play offline, eligible high scores and achievement unlocks are queued locally on your device and synchronized when internet connectivity is available.

When online features are active, the game uses Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Functions to validate and store competitive records:

  • Leaderboards and score submissions: Eligible run completions are sent to a backend Cloud Function that validates run parameters (such as score, highest wave reached, run duration, and run identifier) and updates Daily, Weekly, and All-Time leaderboards.
  • Player search and Following: Players can search for other pilots by callsign prefix or exact player code (#XXXX-XXXX) and maintain a one-way Following list (up to 100 pilots) to compare scores on the Following leaderboard. Following another pilot does not require mutual approval and only displays that pilot's public leaderboard entries.
  • Cloud achievements: Unlocking any of the game's 13 achievements records the unlock in your cloud profile so your trophies persist across sessions and linked devices.

Analytics and usage measurement

Star Faction: Ascension uses Google Firebase Analytics to help Heltain understand game usage, session frequency and duration, player retention, and gameplay progression (such as runs started, runs completed, wave milestones reached, and boss defeats).

  • Installation and device measurement data: Firebase Analytics is not completely anonymous at the installation level; to measure active users, sessions, and retention, the Firebase Analytics SDK may process app-instance identifiers (such as a Firebase App Instance ID or Installation ID), general device and Android OS information, and app engagement data.
  • Custom gameplay event parameters: Custom gameplay events logged by the game do not deliberately contain your Firebase UID, pilot callsign, public player code (#XXXX-XXXX), Google Account email address, or Google account identifiers.
  • No Advertising ID or ad personalization: Collection of the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), ad storage, and ad personalization signals are disabled in the current Android implementation, and the Android Advertising ID permission is excluded from the app. Star Faction: Ascension does not display third-party advertising.

Third-party service providers and security

Star Faction: Ascension uses Google Firebase services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions for Firebase, and Firebase Analytics) as service providers to operate authentication, competitive features, and usage analytics. All network traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Heltain does not sell personal data or share player data with advertising networks or data brokers.

Learn more about Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Data retention and profile deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete your pilot profile in-game, clear the application's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. Cloud profile records, leaderboard scores, Following lists, and cloud achievements are retained while your pilot profile remains active.

In-game deletion: You can permanently delete your Star Faction pilot profile and associated cloud data at any time directly inside the game:

Main Menu → PILOT PROFILE → DELETE PILOT PROFILE

Confirming deletion invokes a backend Cloud Function that deletes your active Star Faction Firebase Authentication account, private and public player profiles, player code reservation (#XXXX-XXXX), Daily/Weekly/All-Time leaderboard entries, Following list, cloud achievements, and score submission history. On your device, in-game deletion removes the account-specific local records and pending synchronization queues associated with that pilot profile, without necessarily removing every unrelated application preference or device setting. To remove all local application files and settings from your device, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the game. If your profile was linked to a Google Account, deleting your Star Faction profile removes its connection to your Google Account and deletes your Star Faction game data, but does not delete or affect your underlying Google Account.

External deletion requests: If you no longer have the game installed, you can review deletion instructions and submit a manual deletion request by contacting support as described on our Pilot Profile Deletion page. Because pilot callsigns and player codes are publicly visible on leaderboards, a player code or callsign alone is not sufficient proof of account ownership, and additional verification may be required before a manual deletion request is completed.

Retention exceptions and provider logging: Deleting your pilot profile removes your active Star Faction Firebase Authentication account and the associated Cloud Firestore game records described above. It does not immediately purge ordinary infrastructure, diagnostic, or security logs maintained by Google Cloud / Firebase, records subject to legal or security retention requirements, or previously collected aggregate Firebase Analytics measurement information, which are handled by Google under its applicable service retention policies.

Children's privacy

Star Faction: Ascension is not specifically designed or marketed for children, and Heltain does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
