Star Faction: Ascension Privacy Policy

Heltain

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Star Faction: Ascension is an Android space shooter published by Heltain. This policy explains how the game handles gameplay records and, when enabled, Google Play Games Services features such as sign-in, leaderboards, and achievements.

Playing the game and signing in

You can play without signing in to Google Play Games. If you use Play Games features, Google handles authentication and provides a player identity for leaderboards and achievements. The game may access your Play Games player identifier, display name, and profile information made available by Google to provide these features.

Gameplay information

The game saves gameplay information such as high scores, highest wave reached, unlocked achievements, and preferences on your device. If Play Games features are enabled and you sign in, eligible high scores and achievement unlocks are submitted to Google Play Games Services. The game may locally retain pending submissions until connectivity is restored.

Leaderboards and friends

Google Play Games provides public and friends leaderboards. When you access these features, the game requests leaderboard entries, including available player names, rankings, and scores. Friends information is subject to Google's permissions and sharing settings. The game may save limited previous leaderboard results on your device to identify changes in rankings between sessions.

Your score and Play Games profile information may be visible to other players according to the leaderboard's visibility settings and your Google Play Games privacy controls.

Third-party services and sharing

Star Faction: Ascension uses Google Play Games Services for optional online game features. Information sent to Google through those features is processed under Google's applicable policies. Heltain does not operate a separate game-account server or an independent friends/leaderboard database for these features.

Learn about Google's privacy practices in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and notifications

Star Faction: Ascension does not currently include advertising or a custom push-notification system for friends' scores. In-game ranking updates may be displayed when you open the game or view the leaderboards.

Storage, security, and retention

Local gameplay records and preferences remain on your device until they are reset, application data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to any Android backup settings. Google Play Games records are retained and handled by Google according to its policies and account controls. Heltain does not have a separate server-side copy of your Play Games records to delete.

Your choices and deletion

You may play without using Google Play Games. You can manage Google Play Games permissions and data through your Google account and Play Games settings. You may clear local application data or uninstall the game to remove locally stored game records, subject to device backup behavior. Removing local data does not automatically remove data held by Google Play Games.

Children's privacy

Star Faction: Ascension is not specifically designed or marketed for children. Access to Play Games features may depend on age, account supervision, and Google's requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change if the game's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when it does.
