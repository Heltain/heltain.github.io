💥 Mayhem Guide

Your goal is to disrupt traffic flow, start physical chain collisions, scatter protesters, and trigger massive pileups before the 3-minute (180-second) round timer runs out.

🐶 Bark Knockdowns 🔥 Contagious Cartoon Fire 📣 Anti-Animal Protests (+500) ✈️ Emergency Plane Crashes ⚡ Overdrive Chains
  • Bark Knockdowns: Barking directly knocks down nearby pedestrians with hilarious slapstick ragdoll tumbles. Accessories like crutches and signs fly into the air!
  • Contagious Cartoon Fire: Chaos Burst shockwaves ignite slapstick flames that spread from pedestrian to pedestrian on physical contact before they stop-drop-and-roll!
  • Anti-Animal Protests: Cause mayhem and citizen groups will march in with picket signs opposing your animal! Knock down or scatter 70% of the group for an instant +500 Disruption Bonus and +25 Mayhem!
  • Re-agitate traffic jams: Barking near blocked vehicles pushes impatient drivers into aggressive breakout swerves!
  • Airborne Catastrophe: Cause genuine traffic chaos (10 collided vehicles for flyover, 18 for dramatic emergency crash) to trigger cinematic aircraft events!
