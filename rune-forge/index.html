Rune Forge merges the intellectual elegance of classic Sudoku with the atmospheric depth of an ancient Norse blacksmith's workshop. In place of plain digits, you weave nine elemental runes into stone and ember, carving order out of chaos across classical rectangular grids and organically shaped irregular territories.

Three Progressive Difficulty Tiers

Rune Forge is structured to welcome puzzle newcomers while presenting a formidable challenge to veteran Sudoku masters:

  • Apprentice (4×4)
    40 levels • 16 cells • 2×2 regular and irregular regions • Runes: Flame, Tide, Stone, Zephyr

    Designed for swift, focused deduction. Learn the interaction between regional boundaries and candidate elimination.

  • Adept (6×6)
    120 levels • 36 cells • 3×2 regular and irregular regions • Runes: + Sun, Glacial

    Deeper complexity requiring multi-step row, column, and territory intersections.

  • Archmage (9×9)
    240 levels • 81 cells • 3×3 regular and irregular regions • Runes: + Thunder, Astral, Nether

    The ultimate trial of logical reasoning. Test your mastery across full-scale regular and labyrinthine irregular boards.

Forge Hammers & Scoring

Every completed level rewards performance with up to three Forge Hammers based on elapsed time against par standards, accuracy, and independent deduction:

  • Par Time Target: Solve swiftly to earn your full complement of Forge Hammers.
  • Master Runesmith Bonus: Solve the puzzle purely on your own merit without activating mistake warnings or hint reveals to secure top score multipliers.
  • Universal Celebration: Each earned Forge Hammer rings the anvil once in the Viking blacksmith's celebration sequence against vibrant elemental effects.

Accessibility & Customization

Play comfortably in any lighting condition, screen size, or playstyle preference:

  • Global Text Scaling: Choose between four responsive text sizes: Small (115%), Medium (140%, default), Large (165%), or Extra Large (190%).
  • Mistake Feedback Modes:
    • Traditional: Pure logic. No error highlights or warnings; your deductions stand unassisted.
    • Show Conflicts: Visually flags duplicate runes within rows, columns, or regions.
    • Auto-Check (Assisted): Real-time verification of placed runes against the true solution.
  • Notes Mode: Pencil in candidate runes within cells to map out complex deductive branches.

Online Leaderboards & Cloud Sync

Rune Forge is built with an offline-first architecture: campaign progression, high scores, and puzzle states are always preserved locally on your Android device.

When online connectivity is available, the game seamlessly synchronizes with Google Firebase backends to offer competitive global leaderboards across three distinct categories:

  • First-Attempt: Pure, unassisted first completions on fresh puzzles.
  • Replay: High-score rankings on replayed puzzles.
  • Assisted: Completions aided by arcane hints or Auto-Check verification.

Online profiles use anonymous guest authentication and randomized fantasy nicknames (e.g. ✦ Thunder Forger 935). You can reroll your random nickname at any time, and you can permanently delete your online profile and leaderboard data directly from the in-game Player Profile menu.
