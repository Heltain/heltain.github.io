Rune Forge (Android package com.heltain.runeforge) is an elemental fantasy logic puzzle game published by Heltain. This Privacy Policy describes how Rune Forge handles player data, local storage, optional Google Firebase cloud services (anonymous authentication, private player profiles, and competitive leaderboards), advertising delivered via Google AdMob, and your options for managing or deleting your data.

Privacy Overview: Rune Forge is built with an offline-first architecture. You can play all 400 campaign puzzles without creating an account, providing an email address, or sharing personal information. When online features are used, the game uses anonymous guest identifiers and randomized fantasy nicknames.

1. Anonymous Player Authentication

Rune Forge does not require you to register with your real name, email address, phone number, password, or third-party social media account.

When online services initialize, the game uses Google Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous guest session. Firebase generates an internal, pseudonymous user identifier (UID) that uniquely identifies your guest session. This UID allows your game progress to synchronize with cloud servers and associates your scores with the online leaderboards without exposing your personal identity.

2. Randomized Player Nicknames

To preserve anonymity while providing community leaderboards, every player is assigned a procedurally generated fantasy nickname (such as ✦ Thunder Forger 935 or ✦ Frost Alchemist 781).

  • No Free-Form Text Entry: Nicknames are generated strictly from curated lists of fantasy adjectives, titles, and numbers. Because players cannot enter arbitrary text, no personally identifiable information (PII) can be shared through nicknames.
  • Player-Controlled Rerolls: You can reroll a new random fantasy nickname at any time inside the game from the Player Profile menu: ⚙ Settings → 👤 Player Profile → 🎲 Reroll Random Nickname

3. Private Player Profile Data

When connected to the internet, Rune Forge synchronizes non-sensitive gameplay statistics to Google Cloud Firestore in a private user document located at users/{userId}, where {userId} is your anonymous Firebase UID.

  • Strict Owner-Only Access: Cloud Firestore Security Rules enforce that private profile documents can only be read or written by the authenticated user whose UID matches the document ID (request.auth.uid == userId). Other players cannot read, query, or view your private profile document.
  • Stored Information: The private profile document contains only aggregate campaign progress: your total earned stars, completed puzzle count, highest unlocked difficulty tier, and active fantasy nickname. It does not contain personal contact data or hardware identifiers.

4. Public Competitive Leaderboards

Rune Forge features optional global competitive leaderboards so players can compare solving performance. Leaderboard records are stored in public Cloud Firestore collections categorized by board size and score category:

  • leaderboards_4_first, leaderboards_4_replay, leaderboards_4_assisted (Apprentice 4×4)
  • leaderboards_6_first, leaderboards_6_replay, leaderboards_6_assisted (Adept 6×6)
  • leaderboards_9_first, leaderboards_9_replay, leaderboards_9_assisted (Archmage 9×9)

Publicly Visible Information: Other players can see only the following leaderboard fields:

  • Your generated fantasy nickname (e.g. ✦ Thunder Forger 935).
  • Your calculated score and cumulative elapsed puzzle time.
  • Total stars earned and highest tier unlocked.
  • Timestamp of your latest score submission.

Leaderboard Categories:

  • First-Attempt: Clean, unassisted solutions completed on a player's first attempt on a puzzle.
  • Replay: Subsequent completions of previously solved puzzles.
  • Assisted: Completions where mistake feedback (Auto-Check) or arcane hints were used during deduction.

5. Local Storage & Offline Play

Rune Forge is fully functional without an internet connection. All 400 puzzle definitions, solutions, active puzzle board states, star achievements, and user preferences (such as text size scaling and mistake warning modes) are stored locally in private sandboxed application storage on your Android device.

When you play offline, no network requests are sent. If you re-establish network connectivity, the game will synchronize your pending campaign stars and eligible leaderboard scores in the background.

6. Advertising & Google AdMob

Rune Forge is supported by advertising delivered through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Advertisements consist of bottom banner ads during gameplay and occasional interstitial ads displayed between puzzle completions.

  • Data Collection by Google: To serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device identifiers, Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP addresses (to determine coarse geographic location), and app interaction/diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • User Consent (UMP SDK): Rune Forge integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. In applicable jurisdictions (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), players are presented with a consent form to manage personalized versus non-personalized advertising choices.
  • In-Game Privacy Controls: You can review and update your advertising privacy and consent choices at any time directly inside the game: ⚙ Settings → 🛡 Ad Privacy & Consent
  • Ad-Free Sunday: Every Sunday based on your device's local calendar, all banner and interstitial advertising is automatically disabled in Rune Forge, providing an ad-free puzzle experience without requiring a subscription.
  • Future Rewarded Ads Disclosure: Optional rewarded video advertisements offering in-game magical boons are planned for future content updates, but are not currently implemented or active in the present release.

7. Data Retention & Cloud Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your Android device until you clear application storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Cloud profile records and leaderboard scores are retained while your anonymous account remains active.

In-Game Account & Data Deletion

You have direct control to permanently delete your online data at any time from within Rune Forge:

⚙ Settings → 👤 Player Profile → 🗑 Delete Online Cloud Data

Selecting this option displays an explicit confirmation dialog. When confirmed:

  • Your private profile document (users/{userId}) is permanently deleted from Cloud Firestore.
  • Your associated leaderboard entries across all board sizes and categories are removed.
  • Your anonymous Firebase Authentication guest credentials are terminated.
  • Your local campaign progress on the device is preserved so you do not lose offline puzzle progress. If you restart the game online, a fresh anonymous guest profile will be initialized.

Manual / External Deletion Requests

If you have already uninstalled the application or cannot access the in-game deletion tool, you may request manual deletion of any server-side records associated with your account by emailing Heltain at heltain.apps@gmail.com. Please provide your in-game fantasy nickname or Account ID as shown in your Player Profile screen.

8. Security Practices

We employ technical measures to protect your information:

  • All network communications between Rune Forge and Google Firebase or AdMob servers use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections in transit.
  • Cloud Firestore Security Rules strictly restrict read and write permissions on private player profiles to the authenticated owner.
  • Heltain does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to third-party data brokers.

9. Third-Party Service Providers

Rune Forge relies on Google LLC for backend infrastructure and advertising delivery. Please review their privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy & Security Documentation
  • Google AdMob & Advertising Technologies

10. Children's Privacy

Rune Forge is a general-audience logic puzzle game. It is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where applicable by local law), and Heltain does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in game features, legal requirements, or technical architecture. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision date.
