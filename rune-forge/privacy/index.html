Rune Forge is developed by Heltain. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Rune Forge stores game progress, settings, and scores on your device. When connected to online services, the game uses an anonymous player identifier and nickname to save profile information and manage online scores. Your nickname and scores may be visible to other players.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Rune Forge uses Google Firebase for online features and Google AdMob for advertising. These services may process device identifiers, IP addresses, usage information, and diagnostic data to provide their services.

You can learn more about Google's data practices in the Google Privacy Policy. Where applicable, advertising privacy choices are available in the game's Settings.

Data Security and Deletion

Online information is transmitted using secure connections. Game data is retained as needed to provide the services described above, unless deleted or otherwise removed under applicable retention practices.

You can delete your online account and associated cloud data through Settings → Player Profile → Delete Online Cloud Data. Local game progress remains on your device unless you separately remove it.

You may also request data deletion by contacting us.

Contact
